# Andrej Karpathy

## Overview

AI researcher and educator. Relevant to this wiki as the author of the LLM Wiki gist
(2026), which articulates the [[compiled-knowledge-base]] pattern this wiki is built on.

## Position in the LLM Wiki gist

- The document is deliberately **abstract** — it describes the pattern, not an implementation.
  Directory structure, conventions, page formats, and tooling are all left to the reader and
  their agent to instantiate together.
- Working setup: agent on one side, Obsidian on the other, browsing edits in real time.
  His framing — "Obsidian is the IDE; the LLM is the programmer; the wiki is the codebase."
- Prefers ingesting sources one at a time and staying involved, over unsupervised batch ingest.
- Places the idea in the lineage of Vannevar Bush's Memex (1945): a private, curated knowledge
  store where the associative trails between documents matter as much as the documents. Bush's
  unsolved problem was who does the maintenance; the claim here is that the LLM does.

## Claims worth tracking

The load-bearing empirical claim is that **maintenance cost, not reading or thinking, is what
kills human wikis** — and that driving maintenance cost near zero is sufficient to keep one
alive. Single-source and unverified; treat as a hypothesis. It is also the claim most directly
transferable to [[decision-engineering]], where a stale [[decision-ledger]] is the equivalent
failure and the same argument applies: the ledger survives only if maintaining it costs near
nothing.
